About CreditCombo

What CreditCombo does, why it exists, and how to get in touch.

What is CreditCombo?

CreditCombo is a rewards optimization tool that helps you estimate which combination of credit cards can return the most value from your spending profile.

  • Model monthly spending categories and run a card combination optimizer.
  • Compare cards, rewards programs, annual fees, and category multipliers in one place.
  • Switch between estimated and minimum valuation modes depending on how conservatively you want to plan.

Who made it?

CreditCombo is built and maintained by Boris Kafidov (online as l2ggy / laggy), a student at the University of Toronto.

The project started as a practical way to compare card ecosystems with transparent assumptions, and has grown into a public tool for anyone trying to make better rewards decisions.
